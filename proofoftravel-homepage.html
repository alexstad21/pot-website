Protocol Overview

An independent audit layer for the travel industry

PoT operates post-issuance and post-reconciliation — only anchoring events after verified operational signals are available. Deterministic, auditable, conservative.

How it works

Four steps from signal to verified proof

Every proof is conservative, deterministic, and verifiable — designed for audit, insurance, and compliance use cases.

01
Event signal received
Operational signals from agencies, GDSs, airlines, and settlement frameworks are captured after issuance and reconciliation.
02
Verification threshold met
Conservative thresholds designed for audit, insurance, and compliance use cases are evaluated before any proof is generated.
03
Cryptographic anchor created
A tamper-resistant reference is written to public blockchain infrastructure — immutable and independently auditable.
04
Proof available for audit
Downstream parties — insurers, auditors, compliance teams — can verify the event without relying on any single system.

Airspace disruption & data resilience
