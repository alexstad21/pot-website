Why consolidators

A neutral source of truth across your downstream network

Consolidators sit at a unique vantage point in the travel value chain. PoT formalises that position — turning multi-source operational agreement into proofs your agencies and partners can independently verify.

Trusted ticket records
Give downstream agencies cryptographic proof that a ticket was issued, flown, refunded, or reconciled.
Reconciliation evidence
Resolve disputes faster with a deterministic, multi-source verification trail.
Insurance & chargeback defence
Anchor proof references that downstream insurers and acquirers can independently verify.
Disruption resilience
During mass cancellations, PoT preserves an authoritative timeline even when source systems diverge.

Integration model

Three lightweight integration paths

All paths are non-invasive. PoT does not modify your booking, ticketing, or settlement records — it produces independent proof references alongside them.

01
Read-only feed
PoT consumes operational signals from your existing back-office systems via a read-only feed or scheduled export.
02
Reference API
Your applications retrieve proof references via a simple REST API and surface them to downstream agencies and partners.
03
Embedded verifier
Optional embeddable verifier widget for partner portals and customer-facing reconciliation tools.

What you keep

You keep your existing GDS, mid-office, settlement, and reporting platforms. PoT does not replace any of them.

What you gain

You gain a neutral, cryptographically anchored audit trail that downstream agencies, insurers, and auditors can independently verify — without exposing any passenger data.
